04 About

About Elena

Based in Spain · Working worldwide

I'm Elena, a Web Designer and WordPress Developer with a background in Industrial Design Engineering and Product Development.

My engineering background shaped the way I work: I start by understanding how things function — the business, the people who use it and what it needs to achieve — and only then move into design. That mix of structure and aesthetics is what I bring to every project.

For more than 6 years I've designed and built websites, apps, brand identities and digital documents for architecture and interior studios, hospitality brands, health and wellness businesses, technology companies, NGOs and sports clubs, working with clients in Spain and abroad.

I handle the whole process myself, from strategy and UX to visual design, WordPress development and SEO On-Page. That means one point of contact, clear communication and a result that looks good and is easy to manage once it's live.
